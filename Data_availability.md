# Data Availability

The 30m forest canopy height maps for Yunnan Province (1986–2023) generated by this code are available as Google Earth Engine Assets. 

Due to the large data volume, the raster files are not hosted on Zenodo. 

To access the assets:
1. Send an email to heli_swfu.edu.cn with your GEE account email.
2. We will share the GEE Asset with your account.
